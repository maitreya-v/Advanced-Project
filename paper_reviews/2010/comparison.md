## Paper-by-paper disposition

### PMID_20925475: An 'integrative neuroscience' perspective on ADHD: linking cognition, emotion, brain and genetic measures with implications for clinical support

Integrative neuroscience review: potential markers and treatment tailoring, with little consensus. Does not demonstrate that online information, diagnosis, coordination, or provider access causes the graph outcomes.

### PMID_19270267: Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder in men and women newly committed to prison: clinical characteristics, psychiatric comorbidity, and quality of life

Selected prison sample adds directly measured adult QOL/comorbidity and emotional/social-function associations. It does not isolate impairment as a cause of QOL, diagnosis as a benefit, or general-population age-32/45 effects.

### BIPOLAR_ADULT: Bipolar Symptoms in Adult Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder: A Cross-Sectional Study of 510 Clinically Diagnosed Patients and 417 Population-Based Controls

Adult cross-sectional symptom overlap and differential-assessment recommendation add partial diagnostic-ambiguity support at age 32. MDQ-positive screening is not synonymous with confirmed bipolar disorder or actual misdiagnosis. Educational/occupational associations strengthen existing partial functional context. Ages 18–40 are stated for controls; the patient age range and exact-age estimates are not supplied. The 2010 article/journal date takes precedence over conflicting website citation export text.

### PMID_20441004: Can we prevent smoking in children with ADHD: a review of the literature

Smoking-risk review: smoking/nicotine outcomes are absent from these graphs. No new edge was introduced and smoking-risk claims were not substituted for symptom severity or general impairment.

### PMID_21206548: Differences between objective and subjective sleep measures in children with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder

Child sleep study, ages 7–12: parent-rated sleep problems correlate with behavior but polysomnography shows no group differences. This preserves partial sleep–behavior support while adding measurement/reverse-direction limitations; it is not a direct contradictory test of the existing actigraphy-duration relationship.

### PMID_21391409: Disparities in ADHD assessment, diagnosis, and treatment

Pediatric disparity commentary adds partial socioeconomic/financial access and treatment-distribution support. Proposed insurance and service barriers are clinical explanations, not measured income interventions, access-effect estimates, or treatment-continuity effects. Adult claims are not upgraded from this paper.

### PMID_21766545: Effectiveness of nutritional interventions on the functioning of children with ADHD and/or ASD. An updated review of research evidence

Nutritional review abstract describes its scope and scientific-validity requirements but supplies no effectiveness conclusion. General nutrition-to-severity remains insufficient at all ages; nutritional intervention and inherited genetic liability remain different constructs.

### PMID_21391408: Geographic analysis of diagnosis of Attention-Deficit/Hyperactivity Disorder in children: Eastern Wisconsin, USA

Geographic study of ages 5–17: recorded diagnosis varies with demographic/location/lead factors. Referral efficiency and mechanisms require further study. Diagnosis distribution is not a direct measure of institutional bias or service access; no blanket bias/referral upgrades. Lead associations augment existing partial exposure context without proving causation.

### PMID_20218799: Neurochemical alterations in women with borderline personality disorder and comorbid attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder

Small MRS comparison of 14 women with both BPD and ADHD and 18 controls. Does not isolate ADHD from BPD, establish executive-function causation, or estimate a causal comorbidity/impairment effect. No standalone status change.

### CAUDATE_HTML: Quantitative MR analysis of caudate abnormalities in pediatric ADHD: Proposal for a diagnostic test

Same 2010 caudate imaging paper as the supplied CAUDATE_MRI PDF. Parsed the abstract; the saved HTML also contains full text. Diagnostic ratio accuracy in a selected child sample is not a new study or evidence on care-access, school-support, or adult diagnosis pathways.

### PMID_21117844: Review of attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder comorbid with oppositional defiant disorder

ADHD/ODD practitioner review reinforces comorbidity screening and condition-specific medication/behavior management recommendations. It supplies no new controlled generic therapy effect or access probability; existing treatment statuses remain partial.

### PMID_20410711: Sex and age differences in attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder symptoms and diagnoses: implications for DSM-V and ICD-11

Same study as AGE_SEX (nihms291682.pdf), ages 7–29. It is not independent replication, does not supply estimates at 32/45, and does not resolve categorical Symptom Type coding or prove gender norms cause detection differences.

### PMID_20513225: Stimulant drug effects on attention deficit/hyperactivity disorder: a review of the effects of age and sex of patients

Stimulant review summarizes randomized efficacy across childhood/adulthood and contextual tolerability. Strengthens existing partial symptom efficacy without exact-age or numeric-strength validation. Well tolerated does not mean zero side effects, and adverse-effect-related adherence was not estimated.

### PMID_20839896: The impact of medications on quality of life in attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder: a systematic review

Systematic review of 25 medication/QOL studies, mostly pediatric and atomoxetine. Adult QOL benefits add partial related-functioning support to age-45 medication → impairment; this is not a separately measured generic impairment outcome or age-45 estimate. Does not validate diagnosis → QOL, side effects → QOL, or behavioral therapy → QOL. No medication → QOL edge was added because the original graphs do not contain one.

### PMID_19482837: The presence of attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder (ADHD) and obsessive-compulsive disorder worsen psychosocial and educational problems in Tourette syndrome

Tourette pediatric sample: ADHD/OCD comorbidity is associated with greater psychosocial/educational problems. Supports selected-population impairment context, but educational support need is not evidence that diagnosis causes accommodations or that accommodations reduce impairment.

### PMID_20660947: [Event-related potentials associated with error detection in adult ADHD--literature review]

Adult electrophysiological review adds cognitive-processing context to existing partial executive/function evidence. Does not establish a controlled executive-deficit → practical-impairment effect or a midlife-specific estimate.

### PMID_20200839: [Population-based diagnostic study to compare the DSM-IV and the Questionnaire for screening Latin American schoolchildren and adolescents for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder]

Single outpatient comparison of two pediatric screening approaches finds no significant detection difference. No diagnostic gold-standard error-rate estimate; does not establish adult criteria-variability effects or resolve the aggregate misdiagnosis-rate coding issue.

## Reports, graph interaction and verification
