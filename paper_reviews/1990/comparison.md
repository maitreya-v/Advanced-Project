## Paper-by-paper disposition

### PMID 2365665: A controlled family history study of Tourette's syndrome, I: Attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder and learning disorders

Tourette-family aggregation is selected-population context; it does not isolate genetic causation or an age-specific adult effect.

### PMID 2289969: A critique of current diagnostic criteria for attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: clinical and research implications

Criteria critique adds diagnostic-context evidence; no causal misdiagnosis-rate estimate or proof of chronological guideline access effects.

### PMID 2251528: Attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: does it affect adults too?

Adult review supports qualified diagnostic masking and identification-to-care pathways. Unspecified medical management is not medication-specific trial evidence; psychotherapy is not proof of a particular behavioral modality; quality of life was not measured.

### PMID 2326123: Attention of hyperactive preschool children--electrodermal activity during auditory stimulation

Four preschool hyperactive subjects, physiological attention comparison; no age-eight or adult causal exposure effect.

### PMID 1974836: Attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder

Review provides childhood care recommendations and symptom/adverse-effect context. General statements on adult persistence do not establish adult drug-specific effects.

### PMID 2228924: Effects of methylphenidate on adolescents with aggressive conduct disorder and ADDH: a preliminary report

Small selected adolescent aggression trial; nonsignificant teacher scales do not contradict all symptom outcomes or generalize to age eight/adults.

### PMID 2135682: Efficacy of methylphenidate among mentally retarded children with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder

Controlled pediatric trial with intellectual-disability selection: classroom/work gains but no significant learning or free-play social gains; qualifies rather than establishes global functional improvement.

### PMID 2387786: Family-genetic and psychosocial risk factors in DSM-III attention deficit disorder

Blinded family study adds partial childhood familial/genetic support; inherited versus shared environmental effects and specific adult age effects unresolved.

### PMID 2074343: Intellectual, academic, and adaptive functioning of Tourette syndrome children with and without attention deficit disorder

Selected Tourette pediatric observational comparison; no manipulation of severity or adult executive-function evidence.

### PMID 2319047: Methylphenidate and baseball playing in ADHD children: who's on first?

Controlled boys aged 7.8–9.9: attention during baseball supports narrower symptom and functional-context benefit near age eight.

### PMID 2228923: Methylphenidate in aggressive-hyperactive boys: I. Effects on peer aggression in public school settings

Controlled aggressive-hyperactive pediatric school trial: classroom benefit, context-dependent aggression/social outcomes, and teacher/parent disagreement limit broad efficacy claims.

### PMID 2228921: Parent-based diagnosis of attention deficit disorder predicts a diagnosis based on teacher report

Parent–teacher diagnostic concordance supplies partial parent-information-to-diagnosis support; it does not measure teacher referral causing parental awareness.

### PMID 2258453: Possible influence of carbamazepine on plasma imipramine concentrations in children with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder

Retrospective imipramine/carbamazepine pharmacokinetics; does not measure adherence or adult outcomes. Suggested withdrawal toxicity is not an observed adherence effect.

### PMID 2196520: Side effects of methylphenidate in children with attention deficit hyperactivity disorder: a systemic, placebo-controlled evaluation

Triple-blind pediatric adverse-effect trial adds child-specific side-effect and discontinuation evidence. Abstract is truncated and repeats sentences; no exact-age-eight estimate.

### PMID 2212053: Situational variability in boys and girls identified as ADHD

No clear measured sex differences in situational variability; does not assess gender norms influencing noticed symptoms and cannot resolve unordered Symptom Type coding.

### PMID 2198308: Social judgment processes in hyperactive boys: effects of methylphenidate and comparisons with normal peers

Social-judgment experiment distinguishes general regulation from specific social cognition; no universal social-function or quality-of-life effect.

### PMID 2254504: The adolescent outcome of hyperactive children diagnosed by research criteria: II. Academic, attentional, and neuropsychological status

Child-to-adolescent follow-up: persistent impairment and age-related changes; no age-32/45 cohort. Does not directly oppose the younger-child cross-sectional age-effects claim on the same outcome/population.

### PMID 2235277: The diagnostic value of cerebellar-vestibular tests in detecting learning disabilities, dyslexia, and attention deficit disorder

Selected learning-disability CV diagnostic associations and proposed mechanism; no relevant graph exposure tested, and mechanistic speculation is not established causation.

### PMID 2273073: The effects of behavioral parent training on families with young hyperactive children

Two uncontrolled preschool parent-training cases support narrower home/family benefit and care-pathway evidence, not a controlled age-eight classroom effect.

### PMID 2266220: The frequency and significance of additional self-reported psychiatric diagnoses in children with attention deficit disorder

Pediatric comorbidity associations; not causal adult comorbidity-to-impairment or misdiagnosis estimates.

### PMID 2342842: The relationship between continuous performance tasks and neuropsychological tests in children with attention-deficit hyperactivity disorder

Attention/test-score correlations in boys aged 6–12; no intervention on severity or broad causal impairment effect.

### PMID 2288166: [Are there sex differences in the manifestation of hyperkinetic syndrome?]

No significant sex differences in measured symptom domains; not a test of gender norms/recognition. Existing categorical-sign limitation remains.

### PMID 2346989: [Changes in the concept of minimal brain dysfunction in modern diagnostic classification and its manifestation in our theory and practice]

Historical diagnostic-classification discussion and hypothetical adult-risk models; not observed adult guideline effects or clinical access effects.

### PMID 2397543: [Preliminary study of traditional Chinese medicine treatment of minimal brain dysfunction: analysis of 100 cases]

Selected historical MBD active-comparator child trial; no placebo, blinding detail, or adult outcomes. Does not justify universal medication benefit, numeric strengths, or inference about nutritional exposure.

### PMID 2346992: [The importance of nonverbal motor examination methods in screening for minimal brain dysfunction]

Screening/motor-learning discussion and social-conditioning caution; no measured causal parental denial/awareness, referral, or adult outcome.
