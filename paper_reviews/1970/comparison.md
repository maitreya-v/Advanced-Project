## Paper summaries

- Schain (1970), PMID 5538099, 11 pages, printed 307–317: clinical findings in 40 children aged 7–13 referred for learning problems. Discusses heterogeneous historical minimal brain dysfunction, differential diagnosis, medication management, and family context. This paper was already reviewed; the new PDF is a different file version, not a new study. Existing age-8 partial support for diagnosis → medication (edge 35), medication → symptoms (edge 40), and medication → side effects (edge 43) remains appropriate. Clinical discussion does not establish the remaining causal claims or adult outcomes.
- Wikler, Dixon and Parker (1970), PMID 5491539, 12 pages, printed 634–645: matched cross-sectional comparison of 24 problem children and 24 controls, with psychometric, neurological and EEG measures. The filename includes 2006, but the article itself is dated November 1970. The previous supplied scan ended at 643; this version restores 644–645. Page 644 adds alternative explanations involving selection and different underlying syndromes, a summary, and a call for longitudinal study; page 645 contains acknowledgments and references. These additions reinforce uncertainty about developmental mechanisms rather than identify a causal effect. They provide neither treatment-effect estimates nor adult ADHD outcomes. Sleep-related EEG abnormalities are not an effect of sleep quality on ADHD symptoms.
- Geller, Cowen and Wolf (1970), PMID 4950109, 7 pages, printed 217–223: experimental lesions and tissue repair in immature rats. Already represented in the baseline corpus. It does not directly validate human ADHD, childhood treatment, or adult graph edges.

The earlier validation also includes Obler (1970), PMID 5523436, which is absent from this new folder. The comparison treats these files as additional/replacement copies, not an instruction to remove earlier evidence.
